A MUD you play in Discord

{{WORLD}}

A persistent world of towns and wilds that you explore, trade and fight in, one slash command at a time. It keeps ticking every few seconds, whether you're looking or not.

{{CTA}}How to play

How to play

Everything happens through slash commands, in any server the bot is in. Moves and attacks are orders: they wait for the next tick and resolve all at once, so a second order before then just changes your mind.

Getting around

/register
Make your character. Start here.
/look
Where you are and who's near, with a picture.
/move
One square, any of eight directions.

Fighting

/attack
Your square first, then the one beside you that way.
/choose
Talk, rob or ignore whoever stops you on the road.

Gear

/equipment
Your character sheet: level, money, health, kit.
/inventory
list, equip and unequip what you carry.

Towns

/town inn
Rest back to full health.
/town recruit
Take the class the town's industry trains.
/town store
list, buy and sell at the local shop.

On the road

/trade
list and buy from a trader you meet in the wilds.
Raiders
They spot you from a few squares off, and the bot DMs you a warning. That's your chance to run. Ruins hide you from them; out on a plaza they see you from twice as far.
Dying
Sends you back to town with a quarter of your money left behind.

On the web

/weblogin
A one-time link to the live map, where you can watch the whole world move.

Classes

Everyone starts out wandering. Towns train their own: find the right one and /town recruit.

Your server

Bring Discordia to your own Discord server. Everyone plays in the same world, whichever server they type in.

  1. Add the bot. Discord asks which server; you need Manage Server there.
  2. Run /settings in that server to pick the channels it plays in, where the daily digest goes, and where its players' deaths and hauls are posted.
  3. Tell everyone to start with /register.
